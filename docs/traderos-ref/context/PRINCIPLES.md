# TraderOS Principles

## Philosophy

TraderOS exists to improve decision quality through disciplined research, systematic learning, and continuous knowledge accumulation.

The objective is not to predict markets.

The objective is to become better at making decisions under uncertainty.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Research Before Execution

Never automate what has not been understood.

---

## 2. Risk Before Profit

Survival enables compounding.

Capital preservation is a prerequisite for long-term success.

---

## 3. Every Trade Requires Reasoning

No decision exists without written justification.

Every trade must explain why it exists.

---

## 4. Every Hypothesis Must Be Testable

Ideas are not knowledge.

Only tested hypotheses become evidence.

---

## 5. Every Failure Becomes Knowledge

Losses are acceptable.

Unexamined losses are not.

---

## 6. Data Quality Beats Model Complexity

Clean observations outperform sophisticated models built on weak data.

---

## 7. Simplicity Wins Until Proven Otherwise

Complexity requires evidence.

The burden of proof belongs to sophistication.

---

## 8. Never Optimize Before Measuring

Measurement precedes optimization.

Evidence precedes refactoring.

---

## 9. The Knowledge Graph Is The Moat

Code can be copied.

Validated experience cannot.

TraderOS compounds accumulated knowledge.

---

## 10. Decision Quality Is The North Star

Every architectural decision must answer:

"Does this improve the quality of trader decisions?"

If not, it should not exist.
